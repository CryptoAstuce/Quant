# Quant VC Demo — Visual Identity

## Style Prompt
Investor-grade product cinema built from Quant's existing night-meadow pixel world: confident, legible, playful without becoming juvenile. UI panels feel like field instruments inside a living settlement; motion alternates between precise data choreography and warm mascot-led reveals.

## Colors
- `#071416` — deep night canvas
- `#0d211b` — meadow panel
- `#e8f2e4` — warm foreground
- `#5fd08a` — kHYPE growth / primary accent
- `#f2c14e` — value / attention accent
- `#2fa4a8` — system / HyperEVM accent

## Typography
- Silkscreen — product labels, scene headers, pixel-world voice
- Space Grotesk — investor narrative, metrics, explanations

## Motion
- Medium-energy editorial push transitions for related product beats
- Blur crossfade for the closing vision
- Snappy data reveals; slower, confident product holds
- Pixel accents move in stepped rhythms; investor claims use smooth easing

## What NOT to Do
- No generic blue-purple SaaS gradients
- No invented APY, TVL, or traction numbers
- No dense dashboard screenshots that cannot be read in two seconds
- No casino, trading, or yield-guarantee language
- No mascot slapstick; Quant remains a credible guide
